
**Phase:** 2 - Protect and Manage
**Framework Mapping:** NIST CSF 2.0 (PR.AA-01, PR.AA-05) | NIST AI RMF (MANAGE 1.1) 

## Purpose

To establish strict boundaries for non-human AI agents operating within the NovaCloud infrastructure and prevent broad data exposure via compromised AI tools


## 1. Service Account Enforcement

All AI integrations (including IDE extensions, CI/CD bots, and ChatOps tools) must authenticate using a dedicated API Service Account (e.g., `svc-ai-github-devmind`). AI tools are strictly prohibited from authenticating via individual human user OAuth tokens or inheriting human IAM roles.


## 2. Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP) for AI

- **Scope Limitation:** AI agents are granted access exclusively to the specific repositories or data lakes required for their approved functions

- **Restricted Environments:** No AI agent may possess read or write access to repositories containing:
    
    - Infrastructure as Code (IaC) (e.g., Terraform, Ansible).
        
    - Environment variable files (`.env`).
        
    - Customer PII databases.
        
- **Permission Ceiling:** Generative AI coding assistants operate with a maximum permission ceiling of `Read-Only`. They cannot possess `Write`, `Commit`, or `Admin` privileges under any circumstances.

## 3. Token Lifecycle Management

- API tokens issued to AI services require automated rotation every 30 days.
    
- All AI service account activity must be explicitly tagged and routed to the central SIEM for anomalous behavior monitoring.

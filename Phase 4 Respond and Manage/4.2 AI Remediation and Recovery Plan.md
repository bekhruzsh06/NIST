

**Phase:** 4 - Respond and Manage  
**Framework Mapping:** NIST CSF 2.0 (RC.RP-01, [RC.CO](https://rc.co/)-02) | NIST AI RMF (MANAGE 4.2)

## Purpose

To eradicate the vulnerabilities that led to the AI incident, safely restore business operations, and perform necessary legal/PR communications.

## 1. Eradication (Specific to the NovaCloud Scenario)

Because the [DevMind.AI](https://devmind.ai/) incident involved the ingestion of `legacy_prod.env` (AWS API Keys), the following must happen before recovery:

- **Immediate Secret Rotation:** DevOps must immediately revoke and rotate all AWS IAM keys present in the exposed `.env` file. Treat the environment as actively hostile until keys are rotated.
    
- **Codebase Scrubbing:** Execute a historical Git purge using tools like BFG Repo-Cleaner to permanently remove the hardcoded secrets from the repository's commit history.
    
- **Secret Scanning Enforcement:** Activate the pre-commit secret scanning hooks (designed in Phase 2) to ensure new keys cannot be committed.
    

## 2. Safe Restoration of AI Services

AI services may only be re-enabled after the vCISO signs off on the following:

- **Contract Verification:** The AI vendor contract has been updated to legally guarantee "Zero-Data Retention" for model training.
    
- **Gateway Active:** The LLM API Data Masking Gateway is actively filtering traffic.
    
- **Access Downgrade:** The AI service account has been re-provisioned with strict `Read-Only` access limited exclusively to non-sensitive repositories.
    

## 3. Communication Plan

- **Internal:** Notify the Engineering department that [DevMind.AI](https://devmind.ai/) is temporarily offline for security upgrades. Provide a timeline for restoration.
    
- **External/Legal:** If customer PII was ingested by the vendor, consult the Legal department to determine if regulatory breach notification (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) is required.
    

## 4. Post-Incident Review (Lessons Learned)

Within 7 days of incident closure, conduct a Root Cause Analysis (RCA) meeting.

- Update the Generative AI Risk Assessment Matrix (Phase 1) with any new threats discovered.
    
- Update the Shadow AI Acceptable Use Policy based on how the engineer bypassed initial controls.


# NIST AI Security Lab: Securing the Generative Enterprise

This lab integrates the **NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0** with the **NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)** and its **Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1)**.

## 🎯 Lab Objectives

By completing this lab, you will be able to:

1. **Govern & Map:** Identify "Shadow AI, inventory AI assets, and map data flows between internal systems and third-party LLMs.
2. **Protect & Manage:** Design AI-specific Identity and Access Management (IAM) controls, data masking pipelines, and Acceptable Use Policies.
3. **Detect & Measure:** Develop telemetry and SIEM alerting rules specific to AI threats (e.g., prompt injection, automated exfiltration).
4. **Respond & Manage:** Create an Incident Response playbook to safely quarantine rogue AI agents without disrupting engineering workflows.

## 🗂️ Repository Structure
<br>
This repository maps directly to the four phases of the lab. Each folder contains the expected deliverables for a complete System Security Plan (SSP) and Incident Response strategy:

* `00_Overview/`: The scenario context and lab instructions.
* `Phase 1 Govern and Map/`: Asset registries and AI risk matrices.
* `Phase 2 Protect and Manage/`: AI IAM policies and technical guardrails.
* `Phase 3 Detect and Measure/`: AI telemetry and anomaly detection logic.
* `Phase 4 Respond and Manage/`: Containment playbooks and recovery plans.
* `Templates/`: Blank Markdown templates for you to practice building your own deliverables.

## 🚀 How to Use This Lab

1. Read `00_Overview/The_NovaCloud_Scenario.md` to understand the business context and the breach you are responding to.
2. Navigate to the `Templates/` folder and copy the blank files into your workspace.
3. Work through Phases 1 through 4, attempting to draft the policies and controls yourself.
4. Compare your work against the completed deliverables in folders `01` through `04`.
